Hi, I’m Lucas.

I’m a robotics maker interested in the space between software and the physical world. This site is my notebook: a place to share what I’m building, what I’m learning, and the things that didn’t work the first time.

Most days you’ll find me sketching mechanisms, tuning motors, or asking a robot to do something it definitely wasn’t designed to do.
